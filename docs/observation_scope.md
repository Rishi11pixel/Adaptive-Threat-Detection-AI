# Phase 1 – Day 1: Observation Scope & Threat Definition

## 1. Observation Scope
The system observes data from a simulated environment, including:
- Movement patterns of objects or individuals
- Sensor readings such as motion, proximity, and noise levels
- Temporal behavior patterns over time

## 2. Normal Behavior
- Predictable movement within designated paths
- Stable sensor readings within expected ranges
- Regular activity during predefined time windows

## 3. Abnormal Behavior
- Movement in restricted or unusual areas
- Prolonged loitering or erratic motion
- Sudden or repeated abnormal sensor spikes
- Activity at unexpected times

## 4. Ethical and Safety Constraints
- No personal identification or tracking
- No offensive or weaponized actions
- All responses limited to observation and alerting
- Human operators maintain control at all times

## 5. Assumptions
- All data is synthetic or simulated
- Initial models may produce false positives
- System prioritizes safety and transparency
